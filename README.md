✦ 97 Art Galaxy

Ideas become visible here.

97 Art Galaxy is a living visual gallery for work created under the 97 creative universe.

It isn't designed to feel like a traditional portfolio.
It is a space where identity becomes visual, stories become experiences, and ideas become something you can see.
From a single graphic to an entire digital world, every piece has a reason for existing.

---

🌌 What lives here?
The gallery moves through different creative dimensions:

IDENTITY
Visual identities, logos, brand systems, and the pieces that help an idea recognize itself.

GRAPHICS
Posters, social graphics, campaign visuals, and individual pieces created to communicate something clearly.

STORYTELLING
Visual narratives where the image is only part of the story.

DIGITAL
Websites, interfaces, digital experiences, and experiments built to make interaction feel human.

WORLDS
Larger ideas — projects that aren't simply a graphic or website, but environments, systems, and experiences with a world of their own.

---

✦ The Gallery
97 Art Galaxy uses four continuously moving visual rows, each representing a different creative dimension.
They move at different speeds and in different directions, creating a gallery that feels alive without demanding attention.

Hover or touch to pause.

Explore naturally.

Click anything that catches your eye.

---

🔎 Explore the Work

Projects can be filtered instantly by category:

"ALL" · "IDENTITY" · "GRAPHICS" · "STORYTELLING" · "DIGITAL" · "WORLDS"

No reload.

Just the work.

🧩 Built to Grow

The gallery is intentionally data-driven.

Projects are controlled through a single "PROJECTS" array.

That means adding new work doesn't require rebuilding the gallery.

Add the images.

Add one project entry.

The gallery handles the rest.

97-art-galaxy/
│
├── index.html
│
└── assets/
    ├── world-within/
    ├── bite-by-bytes/
    ├── identity/
    ├── storytelling/
    └── graphics/

The structure is deliberately simple so the creative work stays the focus.

---

⚡ Lightweight by Design

97 Art Galaxy is built to remain visually expressive without becoming unnecessarily heavy.

The experience uses:

- Lightweight CSS animations
- A capped particle system
- No accumulating particle effects
- Responsive layouts
- Touch-friendly interactions
- Minimal dependencies
- Data-driven project rendering

The goal is simple:

Make it feel alive without making it feel heavy.

---

♿ Accessibility

Motion should never be a requirement for experiencing the work.

97 Art Galaxy respects:

"prefers-reduced-motion"

When reduced motion is enabled, animated gallery movement and particles are disabled.

Images also include meaningful alternative text.

---

📱 Designed for the Real World

The gallery is responsive and designed to work across:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile
- Touch devices

Mobile cards are smaller and the movement adapts to smaller screens while keeping the experience calm and readable.

🛠️ Technology

97 Art Galaxy is intentionally simple.

HTML · CSS · JavaScript

No framework is required for the gallery itself.

The architecture is designed around a small, maintainable codebase that can grow alongside the creative archive.

✧ The Philosophy

A portfolio usually asks:
«What have you made?»

97 Art Galaxy asks something slightly different:

«What did you imagine — and how did you make it visible?»

Every project begins somewhere.

A thought.

A problem.

A feeling.

A story.

A possibility.

The gallery is where those invisible beginnings become visible.
